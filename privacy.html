Purring Meditation Publisher - Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Purring Meditation Publisher is a personal, single-user publishing tool used exclusively to upload our own videos to our own TikTok account, @purringmeditation.

1. No third-party users. This application has no users other than its operator. It does not collect, store, process, sell, or share any personal data from any third party. No visitor data, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking.

2. What data it touches. The tool holds only the OAuth access credentials for our own TikTok account (granted by us, to us) and the video files and captions we create ourselves. These stay on our own computer.

3. TikTok data. Through TikTok's official API the tool accesses only the basic profile information of our own account (user.info.basic) and uploads videos to it (video.upload, video.publish). It does not access any other TikTok user's data.

4. Data sharing. Nothing is shared with anyone. Data goes only from our computer to TikTok's API to publish to our own account.

5. Data retention and deletion. Access tokens can be revoked at any time from our TikTok account settings, which fully disconnects the tool.
